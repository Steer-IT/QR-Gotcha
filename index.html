You scanned an unknown QR code and handed your email to a page you'd never seen before. No company name, no verification — just a tempting offer. That's exactly how a real "quishing" attack starts.

In a real attack this could lead to

  • Your email added to targeted phishing and spam lists
  • A fake login page harvesting your work password next

How to stay safe

  • Treat QR codes like links — don't scan ones you don't trust
  • Check the web address before typing anything into it
  • Never enter work credentials on a page reached via a QR code
  • Real venue Wi-Fi rarely needs your email to connect
